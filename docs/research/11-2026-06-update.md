## B. 학계 델타 (2024–2026, 코퍼스 부재 — grep 0건)

### B1. MAD vs self-consistency — 이질성이 진짜 lever (→ 기존 02 보강)
- **Should we be going MAD?** Smit 외, ICML 2024 (PMLR v235), proceedings.mlr.press/v235/smit24a.html — [peer-reviewed]
  - 인용: MAD "do[es] not reliably outperform other proposed prompting strategies, such as self-consistency and ensembling".
- **Stop Overvaluing Multi-Agent Debate** (orig. "If MAD is the Answer, What is the Question?") arXiv:2502.08788 (2025-02, rev 06) — [preprint]. 증거: 5 MAD방법×9벤치×4모델.
  - 인용: "MAD often fail to outperform simple single-agent baselines such as Chain-of-Thought and Self-Consistency, even when consuming significantly more inference-time computation."; "model heterogeneity … as a universal antidote".
- **변경 2024→2026**: Du et al.(2023 "debate improves") → Smit(2024 replication 실패) → 2502.08788(2025 **heterogeneity** 분리). 코퍼스 02는 heterogeneity 개념 보유하나 "MAD가 self-consistency 못이김" 정량반증 부재.
- **pyreez 함의**: 가치는 cross-model 이질성이지 debate ceremony 아님. host가 단일모델에 debate role 추가 = redundant(harness 룰 재확인).

### B2. self-consistency 한계 — coverage≠selection (→ 02/03 신규)
- **Large Language Monkeys** arXiv:2407.21787 (2024-07) — [preprint]. GSM8K/Llama-3-8B: coverage 95.3%@10k샘플 vs majority-vote ~39.8%.
  - 인용: "majority voting or reward models, plateau beyond several hundred samples and fail to fully scale with the sample budget."
- **Nine Judges, Two Effective Votes** (Apple) arXiv:2605.29800 (2026) — [preprint]. 9모델·7family 패널 n_eff≈2.18, Condorcet gap 22pt, 집계가 gap의 ≤11%만 메움.
- **Are More LLM Calls All You Need?** Chen 외 NeurIPS 2024, arXiv:2403.02419 — [peer-reviewed]: voting "performance first increases but then decreases"(쉬운 query 이득, 어려운 query 손실).
- **Universal Self-Consistency** arXiv:2311.17311 — [preprint]: 표준 SC "is not applicable to free-form answers".
- 소프트집계: CISC arXiv:2502.06233 (ACL 2025 Findings, [peer-reviewed]) — "weighted majority vote based on confidence … reducing the required number of reasoning paths by over 40%"; 단 within-item 상대신뢰만 유효, global calibration은 least effective weight.
- **pyreez 함의**: 유사모델 합의 = 준중복 증거(상관오류). free-form 산출물의 consistency-judge는 다른 family로 라우팅. within-item 신뢰만 가중.

### B3. CoT는 math/symbolic 한정 + overthinking (→ 기존 08 보강)
- **To CoT or not to CoT?** Sprague 외 ICLR 2025, arXiv:2409.12183 — [peer-reviewed]. 100+논문 메타분석.
  - 인용: "CoT gives strong performance benefits primarily on tasks involving math or logic, with much smaller gains on other types of tasks."
- **Do NOT Think That Much for 2+3=?** arXiv:2412.21187 (2024-12) — [preprint]: "overthinking … excessive computational resources … for simple problems with minimal benefit." (역U).
- **변경**: "always add CoT"(2022) → math한정(2409.12183) → reasoning모델엔 redundant·overthinking 해악(2412.21187). 코퍼스 08은 "step by step 폐기" 보유하나 이 두 논문 부재.
- **pyreez 함의**: 워커 프롬프트에 CoT 하드코딩 금지, vendor effort 파라미터 사용(harness 룰 직접 backing). evidence 필드의 "reasoning chain(mechanism→break→consequence)"은 출력 형식 spec이지 thinking 지시 아님 — 구분 유지.

### B4. 과잉규정 정량천장 (→ 기존 10 보강, IFEval 2023 세대에서 정지)
- **How Many Instructions Can LLMs Follow at Once?(IFScale)** arXiv:2507.11538 (2025-07) — [preprint]. 20모델·7provider·10–500제약.
  - 인용 ✓: "even the best frontier models only achieve 68% accuracy at the max density of 500 instructions"
  - 인용 ✓: "bias towards earlier instructions, and distinct categories of instruction-following errors"
- **ManyIFEval** EMNLP 2025 Findings, arXiv:2509.21051 — [peer-reviewed]: joint success ≈ p^N(작은 누락이 지수적 복리).
- **Paradoxical Interference** arXiv:2601.22047 (2026-01) — [preprint]: 제약 추가가 compliance↑이나 task accuracy↓ — "prioritize literal compliance over effective problem-solving."
- **변경**: IFEval(2023, 코퍼스 보유 2311.07911) → 정량천장(p^N, 68%@500) + 과잉규정 반증. 양대 벤더 가이드 재작성("destination 명시, 모델 planning 신뢰").
- **pyreez 함의**: load-bearing 제약 **10개 미만** 유지. 중복줄은 instruction 예산을 갉아 load-bearing 누락 유발(omission이 주 실패모드). auto-inject boilerplate를 host task에 박지 말 것(harness de-dup 룰의 정량 backing).

### B5. 프롬프트 압축은 대체로 free (→ 코퍼스 전무)
- **LLMLingua-2** (Microsoft) ACL 2024 Findings, arXiv:2403.12968 — [peer-reviewed]. MeetingBank/LongBench/GSM8K/BBH.
  - 인용: compression "2x-5x", "even better performance than the original prompt in some cases".
- **pyreez 함의**: rationale·설명 padding·verbose framing은 품질손실 없이 strip 가능 — B4 강화. 위험은 under-length 아니라 load-bearing 불변식 누락. 공격적 압축하되 핵심 제약 보존 + eval 검증.

### B6. 환각=인센티브 + abstention gap (→ 기존 03 보강)
- **Why Language Models Hallucinate** Kalai 외(OpenAI) arXiv:2509.04664 (2025-09) — [preprint].
  - 인용: "Under binary grading, abstaining is strictly sub-optimal."; fix: "Answer only if you are >t confident, since mistakes are penalized t/(1−t) points, while correct answers receive 1 point, and an answer of I don't know receives 0 points."
- **AbstentionBench**(Meta FAIR) arXiv:2506.09038 (2025-06) — [preprint]: "reasoning fine-tuning degrades abstention (by 24% on average)"; 좋은 system prompt가 "boost[s] abstention in practice"하나 근본 미해결.
- **confidence-faithfulness gap** arXiv:2601.07767 (2026-01) — [preprint]: abstention rate가 "remain largely invariant to increasing penalties" — 신뢰 숫자가 refusal을 gate 안 함.
- **Reasoning Models Better Express Their Confidence** arXiv:2505.14489 — [preprint]: 신뢰 *숫자* calibration 개선(33/36 설정) — 단 *결정*(refuse)이 아닌 숫자.
- CoVe arXiv:2309.11495(ACL 2024 Findings, [peer-reviewed], 코퍼스 보유): 검증질문을 "answered independently so the answers are not biased" — 격리가 핵심.
- **pyreez 함의**: (1) 비대칭 framing — `[unverified]`/IDK는 acceptable·neutral, confident-wrong는 penalized. (2) 신뢰 숫자로 abstention gate 기대 금지 — 행동 직접 지시("근거 못대면 [unverified]/drop"). (3) 격리 검증(CoVe). (4) over-flag 안 되게 threshold 보정. → 현 no-lookup discipline + falsifier-decisiveness scale과 정합.

### B7. 비평은 외부신호 있을 때만 — false-positive가 주 비용 (→ 기존 07 보강, adversarial 핵심)
- **LLMs Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet** Huang 외(DeepMind) ICLR 2024, arXiv:2310.01798 — [peer-reviewed].
  - 인용: "LLMs struggle to self-correct their responses without external feedback, and at times, their performance even degrades after self-correction."
- **When Can LLMs Actually Correct Their Own Mistakes?** Kamoi 외 TACL 2024, arXiv:2406.01297 — [peer-reviewed]: "self-correction works well in tasks that can use reliable external feedback".
- **CriticGPT** McAleese 외(OpenAI) arXiv:2407.00215 (2024-07) — [preprint].
  - 인용 ✓: "model-written critiques are preferred over human critiques in 63% of cases"
  - 인용 ✓: critics는 "hallucinated bugs that could mislead humans into making mistakes they might have otherwise avoided"
- Constitutional AI arXiv:2212.08073(코퍼스 보유): critique→revise는 *명시된 written principle* 대조라 작동 — "critic에게 standard를 줘라".
- **변경**: binary "self-correction 되나?(아니오)" → 조건부("외부 피드백/fine-tuned skill: 예; prompted intrinsic: 아니오"). 비평은 trainable skill이고 주 비용은 **hallucinated false-positive**.
- **pyreez 함의(adversarial 워커 핵심 설계)**:
  - DO: 구체·substantiable flaw 요구; explicit standard(task 제약/내부일관성/edge case)에 anchor; steelman-first; **invented objection 패널티**("hallucinated flaw가 missed보다 나쁘다 — 답변 자체 내용이 substantiate하는 것만"); 이질모델 비평가 = 무도구 외부피드백 대용.
  - AVOID: "전부 의심/틀렸다고 가정"(Huang degradation, 정답을 오답으로 flip); objection *수량* 보상(nitpick 최적화); 무도구로 open reasoning *정답성 검증* 기대(불가 — 일관성/가정/누락케이스/반론 *framing*만 가능).
  - **순규칙**: 워커는 rubric+steelman 가진 **targeted reviewer**여야지 contrarian 아님.
